The Story

A Deck of Cards. A Global Stage.

Pete picked up his first trick at five years old in San Diego, California, and has practiced magic every single day since. What started as clips on Instagram became one of the biggest magic audiences on the internet.

His content is fast, family friendly, and built to make people hit replay. The mission never changed: inspire others and spread positivity, one impossible moment at a time.
